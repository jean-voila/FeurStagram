When ScrollGuard or WallHabit is the better choice

  • You use an iPhone. Feurstagram is Android-only.
  • You want one tool for every app: YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram together.
  • You only install apps from an app store and don't want to sideload an APK.
  • You want friction rather than removal: WallHabit's challenge lets you still get in when you decide to.

When Feurstagram is the better choice

  • You're on Android and the problem is Instagram. The Reels tab, the feed and Explore are gone, not covered.
  • You don't want an accessibility service watching your screen to decide what to hide.
  • You want it free and auditable: no premium tier, and every patch is public.
  • You also want ads and telemetry gone, and the option of a Following-only feed.
  • You want to lock it in: the permanent lock stops blocks being turned off in a weak moment.
